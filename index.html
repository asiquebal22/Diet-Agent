Sunday — Full Rest

Recovery
Nothing to prove today.Walk if you like. Eat to plan. Get ready for Monday.
Progression

Reach the top of the rep range on every set, then add the smallest available weight.

Rest periods

2–3 min for compounds; 60–90 sec for curls, extensions, raises and core work.

Deload

When performance drops for two sessions: halve sets and stay 3–5 reps from failure for one week.

The two-week adjustment rule

Compare seven-day average morning weights—not one day's reading.

Down 0.5–1 lb/weekKeep food exactly the same.
Flat for 2 weeksRemove about 150 kcal: less oil, cheese, or sweet potato.
Down over 1.25 lb/weekAdd about 150 kcal, mainly oats, roti, fruit, or sweet potato.
Weekly review

Measure the trend.

Body weight is noisy. Record morning measurements under similar conditions and judge progress from weekly averages and waist change.
